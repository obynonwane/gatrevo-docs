## Plans

| | Starter | Growth | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contacts per month** | 50 | 500 | Unlimited |
| **Campaigns** | 1 active | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Knowledge base docs** | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Team members** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Priority support** | — | — | ✓ |

## Free trial

New workspaces start on a 14-day Growth trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all Growth features. At the end of the trial, your workspace moves to the Starter plan unless you upgrade.

You'll receive a reminder email 4 days before your trial ends (day 10 of the trial).

## Upgrading

Go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Upgrade**. You'll be taken to the billing page to enter your card details. Upgrades take effect immediately.

## Contact limits

Each plan has a monthly contact limit — the number of contacts Gatrevo can enroll across all campaigns in a calendar month. The limit resets on the 1st of each month.

When you're approaching the limit (80%+), Gatrevo shows a warning in the dashboard and notifies you in Slack. When the limit is reached, campaigns pause with status `limit_reached`. Upgrading your plan immediately resumes them.

## Cancelling

Go to **Settings → Billing** → **Cancel plan**. Your workspace moves to the Starter plan at the end of your current billing period. Your data is not deleted.
