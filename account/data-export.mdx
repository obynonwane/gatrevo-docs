title: Data export
description: Export your Gatrevo data for compliance or backup purposes.
---

Gatrevo supports a full data export of all data associated with your workspace, including contacts, campaigns, drafts, and sequences.

## Requesting an export

1. Go to **Settings → Data & Privacy**
2. Click **Request data export**
3. Gatrevo prepares a ZIP file containing your data in CSV and JSON format
4. You'll receive an email with the download link when it's ready (usually within a few minutes)

## What's included

- All contacts and their outreach history
- All campaigns and their status history
- All email drafts (approved, pending, and skipped)
- All sequence enrollments and send history
- Knowledge base documents

## GDPR / compliance

If you need to delete a specific contact's data (right to erasure), go to **Contacts** → find the contact → click **Delete**. This removes the contact and all associated outreach history from Gatrevo.

To add a contact to your suppression list (so they're never emailed again), go to **Settings → Suppression list → Add**.

<Note>
  Data exports are available to workspace admins only.
</Note>
