---
title: Campaign from contacts
description: Run a campaign against contacts already in your Gatrevo contact store.
---

Use this campaign type when you want to reach out to contacts you've already imported or previously worked with, rather than finding new prospects.

## When to use this

- Following up with contacts from a previous campaign
- Targeting attendees from an event you imported via CSV
- Running a different message to a specific segment of your contacts

## Starting the campaign

**In the dashboard:**
1. Go to **Campaigns** → **New campaign**
2. Choose **Use existing contacts**
3. Filter by industry, search by name or company, and select the contacts you want
4. Click **Create campaign**

## Contact status badges

When selecting contacts you'll see status indicators:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No badge | Never contacted — good to go |
| **Enrolled** (amber) | Currently in an active sequence |
| **Replied** (green) | Previously replied to an outreach |
| Greyed out | Bounced or unsubscribed — cannot be selected |

Bounced and unsubscribed contacts are blocked automatically. Enrolled and replied contacts can still be selected — Gatrevo shows a warning count so you can make an informed decision.

## Gate 3 — Review drafts

Contacts campaigns skip Gates 1 and 2 and go straight to email drafting. Gatrevo writes a personalised email for each selected contact.

Review and approve drafts individually or tap **Draft all** to approve everything at once.

<Note>
  Contacts campaigns use your internal Gatrevo contact store, not Apollo. If you're using Apollo as your sequencer, contacts are enrolled via Gatrevo's native sender instead.
</Note>
