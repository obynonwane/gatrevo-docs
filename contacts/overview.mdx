title: Contacts
description: Managing your internal contact store in Gatrevo.
---

Gatrevo maintains an internal contact store — a record of every contact Gatrevo has ever enrolled, along with their outreach history. This is used for deduplication and for running campaigns against existing contacts.

## What's stored

For each contact, Gatrevo stores:
- Name, email, company, job title
- Industry
- Last outreach status (enrolled, replied, bounced, unsubscribed)
- Number of times enrolled
- Deal created flag (if auto-deal is enabled)

## Viewing contacts

Go to **Contacts** in the dashboard to see your full contact list. You can:
- Search by name, email, or company
- Filter by industry
- Sort by any column

## Adding contacts manually

1. Go to **Contacts** → **Add contact**
2. Fill in the contact details
3. Click **Save**

## Contact statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| — | Never contacted |
| **Enrolled** | Currently in an active sequence |
| **Replied** | Has replied to an outreach email |
| **Bounced** | Email address bounced — excluded from future campaigns |
| **Unsubscribed** | Opted out — permanently excluded |

Bounced and unsubscribed contacts cannot be selected for campaigns. This is enforced automatically.
