---
title: Quick start
description: Get Gatrevo connected and run your first campaign in under 10 minutes.
---

## 1. Create your account

Go to [www.gatrevo.com/signup](https://www.gatrevo.com/signup) and sign up. You'll receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account.

## 2. Complete onboarding

The setup wizard walks you through connecting three things:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your CRM">
    Connect HubSpot (or choose "No CRM" to use Gatrevo's internal contact store).
    Gatrevo checks your CRM before every campaign to skip existing customers and active deals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your sequencer">
    Connect Apollo with your API key, or choose "Send via Gmail / Outlook" to use Gatrevo's native email sender.
    This is where your outreach emails will be enrolled and sent from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Click **Add to Slack** and authorise Gatrevo in your workspace.
    Reps interact with the bot via DM or @mention. Gate approvals come through here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Invite your team

Go to **Settings → Team** and invite your reps. They'll receive an email with a link to join your workspace.

## 4. Run your first campaign

In Slack, DM `@gatrevo`:

```
Start a campaign targeting fintech CTOs in the UK for our fraud detection platform
```

Gatrevo will walk you through Gate 1 → Gate 2 → Gate 3 and ask for your approval at each step. Once you approve the drafts, contacts are enrolled and emails are on their way.

<Note>
  Your first campaign is the best way to learn how Gatrevo works. It takes about 5 minutes from request to approved drafts.
</Note>
