## Bot not responding in Slack

**Check the bot is installed:**
Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**. If it shows as disconnected, click **Add to Slack** to reinstall.

**Check you're messaging the right bot:**
Search for `@gatrevo` in Slack. Make sure you're DMing the bot, not a channel called gatrevo.

**Check your plan:**
If your workspace is on the Starter plan and has hit the contact limit, campaigns are paused but the bot should still respond. If the bot is completely unresponsive, contact support.

---

## Campaign stuck on a status

**Gate pending but no Slack message:**
Check that Slack is connected in **Settings → Integrations**. If it was recently disconnected and reconnected, the gate message may have been lost — go to the campaign in the dashboard and approve from there.

**Drafting for a long time:**
Draft generation can take a few minutes for large contact lists. If it's been more than 15 minutes, contact support with your campaign ID.

---

## Emails not sending

**Using Apollo:**
Check your Apollo API key and Email Account ID are still valid in **Settings → Integrations → Apollo**. Apollo keys can expire or be revoked.

**Using native sender (Gmail/Outlook):**
Check your Gmail or Outlook connection in **Settings → Integrations → Native sender**. If the token has expired, disconnect and reconnect.

**Check send preferences:**
Go to **Settings → Preferences** and confirm your send hours and send days are set correctly. Emails are only scheduled within your configured windows.

---

## Contacts showing as bounced incorrectly

If a contact is marked as bounced but you believe the email is valid, you can reset their status. Go to **Contacts** → find the contact → click **Edit** → change the status. Note: the next email send will confirm whether the address is valid.

---

## HubSpot not deduplicating correctly

Make sure your HubSpot Private App token has the `crm.objects.contacts.read` scope. Without read access, Gatrevo cannot check existing contacts and will skip deduplication.

Go to HubSpot → **Settings → Integrations → Private Apps** → your Gatrevo app → verify the scopes are correct.

---

## Can't connect Slack to a different workspace

The Slack OAuth page only shows workspaces you're signed into on `slack.com` in that browser. If the workspace you want isn't showing:
1. Open a new tab and go to `slack.com`
2. Sign into the target workspace
3. Retry the OAuth flow
